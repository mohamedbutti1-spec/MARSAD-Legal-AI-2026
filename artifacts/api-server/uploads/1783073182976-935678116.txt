UAE Contract Law Overview

Article 1: Formation of Contracts
A contract is formed when offer and acceptance meet on a specific matter
and its cause, provided that the contracting parties are legally competent
and the subject matter of the contract is determined and possible.

Article 2: Electronic Contracts
Electronic contracts have the same legal force as written contracts under
Federal Law No. 1 of 2006 on Electronic Commerce and Transactions.

Article 3: Termination of Contracts
A contract may be terminated by mutual agreement, by court order, or upon
fulfilment of a condition precedent agreed upon by the parties.

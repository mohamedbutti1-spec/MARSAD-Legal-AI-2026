Phase 61 — Professional Mentor Engine (PME)

Build on top of the existing Professional Guidance Framework v2.

Do NOT modify Judicial Reasoning Engine.
Do NOT modify Theory Mode.
Do NOT modify ADKG.
Do NOT modify RAG.
Do NOT break existing SPG/PGF workflows.
All changes must be additive and configuration-driven.

Goal:
Transform Professional Guidance from step-by-step workflow guidance into an expert mentorship engine.

For every profession and every workflow stage, add a mentor layer that answers:

1. What should the professional do now?
2. Why is this step important?
3. What happens if this step is missed?
4. What is the most common mistake at this stage?
5. What would an experienced expert do first?
6. What warning signs should the user notice?
7. What documents or evidence must be checked before proceeding?
8. When should the matter be escalated?

Add configuration fields to each profession/stage:

- mentorPrompt
- expertReasoning
- whyThisMatters
- commonMistakes
- warningSigns
- escalationTriggers
- expertFirstActions
- doNotDo
- bestPracticeNotes
- professionalJudgmentNotes

Add UI sections inside each stage:

🧭 إرشاد الخبير
⚠️ تنبيهات مهنية
✅ ماذا تفعل الآن
❌ ما يجب تجنبه
📄 المستندات المطلوبة
📌 متى يتم التصعيد؟
🧠 كيف يفكر الخبير؟

Add a button:

"ماذا سيفعل الخبير؟"
"What would the expert do?"

When clicked, show the top 5 expert actions for that role and stage.

Final output must include:

- Professional guidance
- Expert reasoning
- Common mistakes
- Risk warnings
- Escalation recommendation
- Best-practice checklist
- Professional confidence score
- Verification status

Safeguards:
- Never replace the competent authority.
- Never issue binding legal decisions.
- Distinguish legal requirements from best practices.
- Preserve citation verification.
- Preserve anti-hallucination rules.
- If authority is missing, say so clearly.
- Keep UAE law first when legal analysis is involved.
- Theory Mode remains separate and non-binding.

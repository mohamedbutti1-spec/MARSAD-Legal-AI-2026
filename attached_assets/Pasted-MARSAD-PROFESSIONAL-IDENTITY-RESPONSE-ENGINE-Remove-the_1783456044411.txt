MARSAD PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY & RESPONSE ENGINE

Remove the standalone "Legal Assistance" module entirely.

Replace it with a single dropdown menu:

"Professional Identity"
(Who are you?)

The user selects only one professional identity.

The AI automatically changes:
- reasoning method,
- legal depth,
- terminology,
- procedural guidance,
- educational detail,
- drafting style,
- evidence expectations,
- practical recommendations.

The legal outcome remains legally correct, but the analytical path changes according to the selected profession.
